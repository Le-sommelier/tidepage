# Tidepage privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Tidepage is a PDF reader and editor that works entirely on your device.

## What Tidepage collects

Nothing. Tidepage has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The app does
not have the Android network permission, so it has no way to send data off your device.

## Your documents

The files you open, edit, scan or create stay on your device, in the places you choose. While you
edit, Tidepage keeps working copies and autosaves in its private app storage so that work survives
a crash. Autosaves are removed when you save or discard the changes. Everything Tidepage keeps is deleted
when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Tidepage's storage is excluded from Android cloud backup.

## Camera

Tidepage asks for the camera only when you open the scanner. Pictures are used to build the PDF you
are scanning and are not stored or shared anywhere else. Imported pictures are re-encoded, which
removes their metadata, including location.

## Third parties

Tidepage shares no data with anyone and contains no third-party tracking or advertising code.
